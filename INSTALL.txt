GRT Chrono Importer 1.1.3

SD now matches GRT: it is the sample standard deviation (n-1). Your
chronograph may show a slightly smaller SD for the same shots.

Fixes ladders with uneven step sizes: charge weights are now read from the
ladder steps in your .grtload (or from the sim JSON), so the Sim line plots
and the chart no longer doglegs.

Install:
1. Close GRT.
2. Open %USERPROFILE%\GordonsReloadingTool\plugins\ and delete the old
   GRTChronoImporter folder.
3. Copy the GRTChronoImporter folder from this zip into that plugins folder.
4. Start GRT.
